Who We Are

Working together for a healthier and stronger community.

Titemwane Jenda PUSEPA Charity Organisation (TJEPUCHO) is a community-based charitable organisation committed to improving the lives of people in Jenda and surrounding communities.


Our work focuses on caring for vulnerable people, assisting the elderly and sick, supporting hospitals, promoting sanitation and protecting the environment.


We believe that meaningful change begins when communities come together to care for one another.

Our Work

We are working to address important needs within our communities.

❤️

Support for the Vulnerable

We seek to assist vulnerable members of the community, including elderly people, sick people and families facing difficult circumstances.

🏥

Hospital Support

We support hospitals and health-related community initiatives where assistance is needed.

🌱

Environment

We promote environmental protection, tree planting, responsible waste management and restoration of degraded areas.

🧼

Health & Sanitation

We promote better sanitation, hygiene and healthier living conditions within our communities.

🤝

Community Development

We work with communities to identify challenges and encourage collective solutions that improve people's lives.

🌍

A Better Future

Through community participation and support from well-wishers, we aim to create lasting positive change.

Our Work Is Underway

TJEPUCHO is working toward meaningful change, but there is still much to be done. With the support of individuals, organisations and well-wishers, we can reach more people, support more communities and expand our impact.
