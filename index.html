ABOUT TIJEPUCHO

Building Stronger Communities Through Collective Responsibility

Titemwane Jenda PUSEPA Charity Organisation, commonly known as TIJEPUCHO, is committed to promoting human well-being and strengthening communities in responding to social, health, sanitation and environmental challenges.

We believe sustainable development begins with communities themselves. Our approach therefore emphasises participation, compassion, accountability and locally relevant solutions.

Our Mission

To mobilise people, resources and partnerships towards initiatives that promote human dignity, support vulnerable populations, improve community well-being and advance environmental stewardship.

OUR VISION

A Healthier, More Resilient and Sustainable Future

We envision communities where individuals can live with dignity, vulnerable people receive meaningful support, environmental resources are responsibly protected and citizens are empowered to participate in sustainable development.
