WHO WE ARE

Compassion in Action.
Purpose With Impact.

TJEPUCHO is a community-oriented charitable organisation dedicated to responding to challenges that affect human dignity, public well-being and environmental sustainability.

Through community engagement, partnerships and targeted initiatives, we seek to transform identified needs into meaningful and sustainable action.

ABOUT TJEPUCHO

Building Stronger Communities Through Collective Responsibility

Titemwane Jenda PUSEPA Charity Organisation, commonly known as TJEPUCHO, is committed to promoting human well-being and strengthening community capacity to respond to social, health and environmental challenges.

We believe sustainable development begins with communities themselves. Our approach therefore emphasises participation, compassion, accountability and locally relevant solutions.

Our Mission

To mobilise people, resources and partnerships towards initiatives that promote human dignity, improve community well-being, support vulnerable populations and advance environmental stewardship.

OUR VISION

A Healthier, More Resilient and Sustainable Future

We envision communities in which individuals can live with dignity, vulnerable people receive meaningful support, environmental resources are responsibly protected, and citizens are empowered to participate in sustainable development.
